Habit Tracker User Guide

A complete technical and operational guide to all features in Habit Tracker: local Drift SQLite storage, multi-target calculation engines, background isolate focus timer, ambient audio, habit shields, RPG gamification, 28 achievement badges, interactive notifications, home screen AppWidgets, and midnight rollover workers.

1. Architecture & Local-First Privacy

Habit Tracker is built from the ground up on Clean Architecture with unidirectional reactive data flow powered by Flutter and Riverpod.

  • Reactive Drift SQLite Database: All tables (Habits, Logs, Categories, Shields, Gamification, Achievements) are managed locally on your device with type-safe DAOs and sub-millisecond queries.
  • Zero Telemetry & Zero Ads: The app contains no remote tracking SDKs, analytics beacons, or third-party network calls. Your personal routines remain 100% private.
  • Full Offline Capability: Every single feature, including timer sessions, streak calculations, and badge unlocks, operates offline without needing an active internet connection.

2. Core Habit Tracking & Daily Dashboard

The Daily Tracker screen acts as your primary dashboard for reviewing intentions, logging completions, and maintaining momentum.

Dashboard Features

  • Interactive Date Stepper: Jump backward to review past logs, inspect historical adherence with visual warning banners, or jump directly back to today.
  • Completion Progress Ring: Live circular progress indicator calculating today's completion percentage and total XP earned today.
  • Category Filter Chips: Instant filtering across Health & Fitness, Learning, Mindfulness, Productivity, or custom user categories.
  • Pinned Habit Priority: Pinned habits always stay at the top of your list for immediate access.
  • 48dp Tap Targets: Designed for ergonomic, one-handed check-ins.

Recurrence Schedules

Habit Tracker supports four flexible frequency types to accommodate diverse real-world routines:

  • Daily: Scheduled for every calendar day. Unlogged days reset streaks unless protected by a Habit Shield. Current in-progress days are preserved while the day is active.
  • Weekly Target: Configure a target number of completions per week (e.g. 3 times per week). Evaluated strictly on canonical Monday through Sunday week boundaries. Streaks increment in units of completed weeks.
  • Custom Days: Designate specific active weekdays (e.g. Mon, Wed, Fri). Non-scheduled days are automatically skipped by the streak engine without breaking active streaks.
  • Interval (Every N Days): Recurring interval rhythms (e.g. Every 2 days or Every 3 days) anchored to your habit creation date.

Target Calculation Engines

Four specialized target engines ensure accurate logging for any habit type:

  • Boolean Targets: Binary 1-tap completion checkmark with system haptic feedback (e.g. Take Morning Vitamins).
  • Numeric Targets: Quantitative goals with customizable units (e.g. 2500 ml, 20 pages, 50 pushups), dynamic delta steppers (+5, +10, +250ml), and a direct numerical keypad dialog for exact manual entry.
  • Focus Timer Duration: Dedicated focus duration goals (e.g. 45 minutes Deep Work) with 1-tap launch into the Zen timer.
  • Sub-Day Time Slots: Divides a habit into multiple time-windowed intervals (e.g. Slot #1 Morning 8-10am, Slot #2 Afternoon 1-3pm, Slot #3 Night 8-10pm) with individual segment check-ins.

3. Circular Focus Timer, Zen Mode & Audio

The Circular Focus Timer provides an immersive, distraction-free environment for deep work, study, reading, exercise, and meditation.

Timer Features & Background Isolate

  • Persistent Background Isolate: Operates inside an independent background isolate and binds to an Android foreground service. Screen lock, multitasking, or sleep states do not interrupt your session.
  • Lock-Screen Notification Controls: Pause, resume, or stop the countdown directly from the Android notification shade or lock-screen.
  • Quick Delta Steppers: Add or subtract 5 or 10 minutes on the fly (+5m, -5m, +10m, -10m) without restarting.
  • Automatic Session Logging: Completed sessions write elapsed duration directly into the habit history database and award XP.

Ambient Sound Generator

An integrated ambient sound player helps you drown out background noise and maintain focus. Built-in soundscapes include:

  • Rain: Gentle rainfall soundscape.
  • White Noise: Constant neutral static for concentration.
  • Cafe: Ambient coffee shop background chatter.
  • Forest: Natural woodland atmosphere.

Do Not Disturb (DND) Automation

When granted the Android Notification Policy access permission, Habit Tracker automatically silences phone notifications upon starting a focus session and restores your previous sound profile when the timer ends.

4. Habit Shields & Streak Protection

Life happens. Sick days, intense travel, or emergencies shouldn't wipe out months of consistent discipline. Habit Tracker features an intelligent Habit Shield banking engine.

Shield Banking Rules

  • 14-Day Consistency Earning: For every 14 distinct days a habit is completed, you earn 1 Habit Shield in your bank.
  • Auto-Consume on Midnight Rollover: Missed days are automatically evaluated at 00:01 and frozen if you have shields available.
  • Manual Retroactive Freezing: Protect any missed date manually directly from the Habit Detail heatmap or Week Matrix.
  • Shield Bank Cap: Configurable maximum bank capacity to keep incentives balanced.
Streak Math Guarantee

When a day is shielded, the streak engine preserves your unbroken chain. For example, logging Monday, shielding Tuesday, and logging Wednesday yields an unbroken 3-day active streak.

5. RPG Progression, XP & 28 Badges

Build momentum through game-like progression. Every completed action awards XP, scales your level, and unlocks milestone achievements.

Base XP Calculation

  • Check-in: +15 Base XP per completed habit.
  • Numeric Delta: Scaled proportionally by completion percentage (up to +20 Base XP).
  • Focus Timer: +1 XP per focused minute (up to +45 Base XP per session).

Streak Multipliers

Maintaining consecutive streaks scales all earned XP:

Consecutive Streak XP Multiplier Status Tier
0 – 2 Days 1.00x Standard
3 – 6 Days 1.10x Sparking
7 – 13 Days 1.25x Ignited
14 – 29 Days 1.50x Blazing
30+ Days 2.00x Unstoppable

Quadratic Level Threshold Curve

Level progression follows a quadratic curve: Level L requires 100 × L² cumulative XP. Player titles advance through:

  • Novice (Levels 1–3): Getting started with initial consistency.
  • Apprentice (Levels 4–7): Developing stable daily routines.
  • Adept (Levels 8–14): Strong momentum with multi-category adherence.
  • Pathfinder (Levels 15–24): Master of long-term habit streaks and focus work.
  • Grandmaster (Level 25+): Unshakeable mastery over daily discipline.

6. Analytics, 7-Day Matrix & Monthly Heatmaps

Habit Tracker provides deep visibility into your daily and monthly consistency through clean visual charts and adherence matrices.

7-Day Week Matrix

  • Monday–Sunday Structure: High-density weekly overview showing all active habits.
  • Matrix States: Completed (solid circle), Shielded (cyan shield ring), Missed (dim outline), Off-Day (dash).
  • Quick Long-Press Freeze: Long-press any day cell to freeze or unfreeze dates on the fly.
  • Weekly Adherence Bar: Real-time weekly percentage and completion ratio.

Habit Detail Screen & 10-Dot Progress

Opening any habit card reveals comprehensive performance metrics and historical heatmaps:

  • Monthly Calendar Heatmap: Full calendar view of all completed, shielded, and missed days in the month.
  • 10-Dot Progress Bar: Visual 10-segment milestone tracker showing progress toward your next consistency tier.
  • Streak & Rate Stats: Current streak, all-time best record, total completions, and completion percentage rate.
  • Habit Controls: Edit frequency, customize step deltas, pin to top, archive to preserve history, or delete.

Adherence Trend Curves & Consistency Heatmaps

The dedicated Analytics tab aggregates overall routine performance:

  • Dynamic Spline Curve: Visual trend line tracking daily adherence percentages over 7-day and 30-day windows.
  • Monthly Activity Heatmap: 4-level density grid illustrating low, moderate, high, and perfect consistency days.
  • Focus Time Totals: Total accumulated focus hours and minutes across all timer sessions.
  • Category Adherence Distribution: Compare consistency across your physical, mental, and professional habits.

7. Notifications, Reminders & Actions

Habit Tracker integrates deeply with Android system notifications to ensure you never forget your intentions.

  • Per-Habit Reminder Scheduling: Set custom daily reminder times for each individual habit.
  • Exact Alarm Execution: Uses Android exact alarms (`exactAllowWhileIdle`) so reminders trigger precisely even during deep sleep (Doze mode).
  • Interactive Notification Action Buttons: Check-in directly from the notification shade:
    • Mark Done: Instantly completes the habit and awards XP without opening the app.
    • +5 / +10 / +Delta: Adds numeric increment directly from notification.
    • View Habit: Deep-links directly to the habit detail screen.
  • Dedicated Channels: Clean Android notification channel separation for Habit Reminders, Focus Timer Service, and Midnight Rollover Alerts.

Home Screen AppWidgets (4 Types)

Stay on top of your habits directly from your Android home screen with 4 specialized AppWidgets synchronized via SharedPreferences and native method channels:

  • 1. Daily Focus Widget: Glanceable overview displaying daily completion percentage ring, completed habit count, best streak, today's focus minutes, and earned XP.
  • 2. Today's Habits Checklist Widget: Interactive scrollable list of today's habits with 1-tap check-in buttons and pinned habit prioritization.
  • 3. Streaks at Risk Widget: Highlights habits scheduled today that are uncompleted to protect active streak chains.
  • 4. RPG XP & Mastery Widget: Displays current player level, title badge, XP progress bar, and next milestone badge progress.

Dynamic Launcher Shortcuts

Long-press the Habit Tracker icon on your home screen or app launcher to access dynamic shortcuts for your top pinned habits, launching directly into check-ins or focus sessions.

Midnight Rollover Background Worker

An automated background task executes every night at 00:01:

  • Evaluates streak risk for yesterday's uncompleted habits.
  • Automatically applies available bank shields if Auto-Consume is enabled.
  • Recalculates active streaks and clears day caches.
  • Refreshes all home screen AppWidgets for the new day.
