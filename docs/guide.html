Habit Tracker User Guide

Learn how each calculation engine, focus tool, streak freeze mechanism, and gamification system works under the hood.

1. Core Habit Tracking

Habit Tracker is built on clean unidirectional architecture using Flutter, Riverpod, and a reactive Drift SQLite database. Everything is saved locally with sub-millisecond query performance and zero remote network requests.

Habit Frequency Types

When creating a habit, you can configure four distinct recurrence schedules:

  • Daily: Scheduled for every single day. A missed day will break the current streak chain unless protected by a Habit Shield.
  • Weekly Target: Specify an adherence target per canonical ISO week (Monday through Sunday), such as 3 times per week. Streaks increment in units of completed weeks.
  • Custom Days: Specify exact days of the week (e.g. Mon, Wed, Fri). Non-scheduled days are automatically skipped and never break your streak.
  • Interval (Every N Days): Specify recurring interval rhythms (e.g. Every 2 days).

Target Types

  • Boolean: Binary 1-tap completion checkmark (e.g. *Take Vitamins*).
  • Numeric: Track quantitative goals with customizable units and dynamic delta increment chips (e.g. *Drink 2500 ml*, *Read 30 pages*).
  • Timer: Dedicated focus duration goals (e.g. *45 minutes Deep Work*).
  • Sub-Day Slots: Divide a habit into multiple time-windowed daily intervals (e.g. *Morning 8-10am*, *Afternoon 1-3pm*, *Night 8-10pm*).

2. Circular Focus Timer & Zen Mode

The Circular Focus Timer is designed for deep work, meditation, pomodoro sessions, and exercise. It eliminates all interface clutter, enabling you to enter a distraction-free flow state.

⏱️ Persistent Background Service
The timer runs in an isolated Android background service with a persistent foreground notification. Multitasking, switching apps, or turning off the screen will not interrupt your session.

Timer Features

  • Ambient Circular Progress Ring: Renders remaining session duration with smooth interpolation.
  • Quick Delta Increments: Add or subtract 1m or 5m on the fly without restarting your session.
  • Notification Controls: Pause, resume, and stop sessions directly from the Android lock-screen or notification shade.
  • Automatic Completion Logging: When the countdown finishes, elapsed focus time is automatically logged into the habit history database and awards corresponding XP.

3. Habit Shields & Grace Days (Streak Freeze)

Real life involves unexpected disruptions—illness, travel, or family emergencies. Habit Shields protect your streaks against unavoidable missed days so you never suffer the demotivating "what-the-hell" effect from a broken 50-day streak.

How Shields Work

  • Starter Gift: Every user receives 1 starter shield automatically.
  • 14-Day Consistency Banking: You earn 1 additional shield for every 14 days of unbroken consistency across your habit routine.
  • Configurable Bank Capacity: Store up to 3 shields by default (customizable from 1 to 10).
  • Streak Continuity Preservation: When a shielded day is encountered, the streak engine preserves the unbroken chain.
  • Authentic Historical Integrity: A shielded day keeps the streak alive but does not falsely inflate your actual check-in counts or completion metrics.
🛡️ Auto vs Manual Protection
Auto-Consume: The midnight rollover task automatically freezes active streaks that were missed.
Manual Freeze: Tap any past date in the Habit Detail calendar or long-press in the Week Matrix to freeze or unfreeze a specific date.

4. RPG Gamification & Mastery Progression

Habit Tracker incorporates RPG elements to make discipline intrinsically rewarding. Completing habits awards Experience Points (XP), levels up your player rank, and unlocks milestones.

Base XP Formula

  • Boolean Habit: +15 Base XP per completion.
  • Numeric Habit: Scaled proportionally to target completion (up to +20 Base XP).
  • Focus Timer Habit: Scaled by duration (+1 XP per minute, up to +45 XP per session).

Streak Multipliers

Maintaining consecutive streaks scales your earned XP:

Consecutive Streak XP Multiplier Status
0 – 2 Days 1.00x Standard
3 – 6 Days 1.10x Sparking
7 – 13 Days 1.25x Ignited
14 – 29 Days 1.50x Blazing
30+ Days 2.00x Unstoppable

Quadratic Level Threshold Curve

Level requirements follow a quadratic progression: Level \(L\) requires \(100 \times L^2\) cumulative XP. This ensures early levels feel fast and motivating while higher masteries reflect true long-term dedication.

5. Week Matrix & Completion Analytics

The Week Matrix view provides a high-density 7-day visualization conforming to the ISO 8601 standard (weeks start on Monday and conclude on Sunday).

Matrix Cell States

  • Completed (Solid Checkmark): Habit target successfully logged for that date.
  • Shielded (Cyan Shield Icon): Protected grace day preserving consecutive streak.
  • Missed (Dim Border): Scheduled past day that was not completed.
  • Non-Scheduled (Dash): Day off based on custom schedule.

6. Widgets, Shortcuts & System Integration

Habit Tracker integrates deeply with Android system features to make logging instant from anywhere on your device.

  • Android AppWidgets: Home screen widgets synchronized via SharedPreferences and background method channels.
  • Dynamic App Shortcuts: Long-press the app launcher icon to instantly check-in or focus on your top pinned habits.
  • Midnight Rollover Background Task: Auto-evaluates streak risks, consumes shields if enabled, and resets daily widgets at 00:01.
