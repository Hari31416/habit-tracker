App Showcase

Explore the clean Material 3 interface optimized for one-handed operation and clear visual hierarchy.

Daily Routine Tracker

The central dashboard gives you an immediate overview of daily progress with an interactive circular progress indicator, quick date stepper, category filter chips, and 48dp tappable check-in targets.

  • Completion Ring: Live daily percentage and cumulative XP earned.
  • Target Support: Checkbox, numeric delta steppers (+5, +10), and sub-day slots.
  • Quick Focus: One-tap launch directly into deep work timer sessions.

7-Day Adherence Matrix

View full weekly adherence across all active habits on an ISO Monday through Sunday grid. Quickly identify completion patterns, protected shield days, and scheduled days off.

  • 7-Day Adherence: Monday to Sunday consistency grid with clear visual status.
  • Shield Indicators: Clear visual cues for frozen grace days.
  • Quick Action: Long-press any date cell to apply or remove streak shields.

Circular Focus Timer

Distraction-free environment built for deep work, study, reading, and meditation. Runs in an isolated background service with persistent notifications.

  • Ambient Ring: Smooth countdown with time remaining display.
  • Step Adjusters: Instantly add or subtract duration (+5m, -5m).
  • Auto-Logging: Completed session duration is stored directly into habit history.

Habit Detail & Calendar

Drill down into individual habit performance with full monthly heatmaps, streak milestones, target definitions, and historical logs.

  • Monthly Heatmap: Visual record of completions, shields, and missed days.
  • Streak Statistics: Current streak, best streak record, and consistency rate.
  • Habit Management: Edit schedule, archive, or delete habits cleanly.

Habit Shield Bank

Protect consistency from unforeseen disruptions. Earn 1 shield for every 14 days of unbroken habit logging up to your configurable bank limit.

  • 14-Day Banking: Visual progress bar showing days until your next shield.
  • Auto-Consume: Optional automatic streak freeze on midnight rollover.
  • Authentic Metrics: Shields preserve streaks without falsifying completion counts.

Adherence Analytics & Heatmaps

Comprehensive adherence metrics, weekly and monthly trend curves, category breakdown distribution, and monthly consistency heatmaps.

  • Adherence Curves: Weekly completion trend charts and scorecards.
  • Category Breakdown: Visual distribution across health, learning, and work.
  • Total Volume: Total logged hours, check-ins, and active streak counts.

RPG Mastery & Badges

Experience point progression, rank titles from Novice to Grandmaster, streak multiplier boosts, and unlockable achievement badges.

  • Quadratic XP: Balanced leveling curve that values consistent discipline.
  • Streak Multiplier: Up to 2.0x XP multiplier for 30+ day streaks.
  • Achievement Showcase: Over 20 milestone badges rewarding dedication.
