App Showcase

Explore the clean Material 3 interface optimized for one-handed operation and clear visual hierarchy.

Daily Routine Tracker

The central dashboard gives you an immediate overview of daily progress with an interactive circular progress indicator, quick date stepper, category filter chips, and 48dp tappable check-in targets.

  • Completion Ring: Live daily percentage (80%) and cumulative XP earned (+100 XP).
  • Target Support: Checkboxes, numeric delta steppers (+5, +10), and custom goal units.
  • Quick Focus: One-tap launch directly into deep work timer sessions.

7-Day Adherence Matrix

View full weekly adherence across all active habits on an ISO Monday through Sunday grid. Quickly identify completion patterns, protected shield days, and daily completion volume charts.

  • 7-Day Adherence: Monday to Sunday consistency grid with clear visual status dots.
  • Completions Chart: Daily completion bar graph for routine momentum.
  • Quick Action: Long-press any date cell to apply or remove streak shields.

Circular Focus Timer

Distraction-free focus sessions integrated directly with habit goals. Features ambient noise playback, Do Not Disturb automation, and a persistent foreground notification service.

  • Background Service: Uninterrupted countdown when multitasking or screen is locked.
  • Ambient Sounds: Forest Birds, Rain, White Noise, and Cafe soundscapes.
  • Delta Adjustments: Add/subtract minutes dynamically (+5m, -5m) on the fly.

Habit Detail & Streak Freeze

Deep-dive into individual habit performance with interactive calendar heatmaps, streak freeze status banners, 10-dot milestone progress indicators, and embedded focus controls.

  • Streak Freeze Banner: Clear visual confirmation when a day is protected by a shield.
  • 10-Dot Progress: Milestone tier indicator showing progress toward routine consistency.
  • Integrated Focus: Launch timer sessions directly from habit cards with preset goal chips.

Habit Shield Banking

Protect your streaks when unexpected events happen. Bank shields through consistent tracking and freeze missed days without breaking your streak.

  • 14-Day Banking: Earn 1 shield for every 14 distinct days completed.
  • Yesterday Protect: 1-tap protection for missed habits from the previous day.
  • Auto-Consume Rollover: Missed days freeze automatically if shields are available.

Check-In Reflections & Wellbeing

Capture qualitative context when completing habits. Reflect on energy levels and emotional state to see how habits influence your day.

  • Energy Rating: 5-point physical and mental stamina scale (Low to Peak).
  • Mood Tags: Tag check-ins with Energized, Happy, Calm, Focused, Tired, or Stressed.
  • Micro-Notes: 120-character brief contextual annotations saved to habit history.

Google Health Connect Auto-Sync

Seamlessly automate habit check-ins for 7 core physical health metrics synced directly from Google Fit, smartwatches, and fitness trackers.

  • 7 Health Metrics: Steps, Active Exercise, Move Minutes, Distance, Calories, Hydration, and Sleep.
  • Zero-Touch Check-Ins: Automatically logs numeric values and marks habits complete upon reaching target.
  • Background Reconciliation: WorkManager workers sync periodically and reconcile late-night activity over the last 3 days.

Multi-Dimensional Analytics

Track long-term habit velocity through rolling adherence curves, completion rates, top habit rankings, and wellbeing correlation insights.

  • Trend Curves: 7-day and 30-day rolling adherence trajectory spline.
  • Wellbeing Correlation: Energy comparison on completed vs. missed days.
  • Monthly Heatmap: GitHub-style 4-level density grid showing routine momentum.

Mastery & Badges

Earn XP for daily check-ins, unlock titles from Novice to Grandmaster, boost rewards with streak multipliers, and collect 28 milestone achievement badges.

  • Quadratic XP: Balanced leveling curve that rewards long-term discipline.
  • Streak Multiplier: Up to 2.0x XP multiplier for 30+ day streaks.
  • 28 Milestone Badges: Filter by Streaks, Volume, Diversity, and Perfection.

Data Portability & Secure Backups

Take full control of your habit records with zero-knowledge AES-256 encrypted backups, Gzip compression, CSV exports, and deterministic multi-device sync merging.

  • AES-256 Encryption: Zero-knowledge client-side encryption with 100,000 PBKDF2 iterations and passkey generation.
  • Gzip Archive Compression: Reduces backup file sizes by up to 90% (.json.gz).
  • Deterministic Sync: Last-Write-Wins (LWW) conflict resolution for lossless multi-device merging.
  • CSV Export: Standard RFC 4180 tabular spreadsheets for Excel, Sheets, and Python data pipelines.
