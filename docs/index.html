●  Tour

Take the app for a spin

Thirteen real screens — from the daily tracker to encrypted backups. Tap a pill to explore.

Daily tracker & categories

Completion ring, date stepper, filter chips with live counts, archived management, and 48dp one-handed check-ins.

  • Completion ring: live % + XP earned today.
  • Every target: checkboxes, steppers, Clean badges, custom units.
  • Archived chip: review, restore, or pause with safeguards.

Habit stacking & flow player

Chain habits into sequences and execute them in a guided full-screen player with timers, prompts, and bonus XP.

  • Reorderable builder: drag steps, icons, colors, time windows.
  • Guided player: “After X → Next: Y”, countdowns, skip, celebration.

Abstinence & urge surfer

Live clean-time counter, milestone ladder (24h → 1yr), compassionate resets, and 4-4-4-4 box breathing for cravings.

  • Passive tracking: clean days accrue automatically.
  • Urge surfer: 2-min breathing with chimes + affirmations.

Elastic goals & bad-day mode

Mini / Base / Elite tiers (e.g. 5 / 20 / 40 pages) so low-energy days still count. Mini saves your streak (+5 XP).

  • Mini → Elite: 5 / 20 / 35 XP, boosted by streak multipliers.
  • One-tap bar: tier selector + live badges on every card.

7-day adherence matrix

ISO Monday–Sunday grid with completions chart. Long-press any cell to freeze or unfreeze a day.

  • Status dots: done, shielded, missed, off-day.
  • Volume chart: daily completions at a glance.

Circular focus timer

Deep-work countdown with ambient sound, DND automation, notification controls, and auto-logged sessions.

  • Background service: keeps counting with screen locked.
  • Soundscapes: Forest, Rain, White Noise, Cafe.

Habit detail & streak freeze

Calendar heatmap, freeze banner, 10-dot milestone bar, stats, and embedded focus controls.

  • Freeze banner: clear shield status per date.
  • Quick focus: preset chips launch timer instantly.

Shield bank

Earn 1 shield per 14 consistent days. Protect yesterday with one tap, or let midnight rollover auto-freeze.

  • 14-day banking: live progress to next shield.
  • 3 ways to apply: auto, detail calendar, matrix long-press.

Reflections & wellbeing

Capture energy (1–5), mood tags, and a 120-char note right after check-in. Saved to history,Skip anytime.

  • Energy scale: Low → Peak physical + mental.
  • Moods: Energized, Happy, Calm, Focused, Tired, Stressed.

Health Connect auto-sync

Link steps, exercise, move minutes, distance, calories, hydration, and sleep. Habits complete themselves.

  • Zero-touch: auto-logs values, marks complete at target.
  • Reconciliation: backfills the last 3 days of late-night activity.

Analytics & heatmaps

Rolling 7/30-day curves, top-habit rankings, energy correlation (done vs. missed days), and GitHub-style monthly heatmap.

  • Trend splines: adherence trajectory over time.
  • Wellbeing link: do habits raise your energy?

Mastery & badges

Quadratic XP (100 × L²), titles Novice → Grandmaster, streak multipliers to 2.0×, and 33 filterable achievement badges.

  • Multipliers: 1.0× → 1.1× → 1.25× → 1.5× → 2.0×.
  • Badges: streaks, volume, diversity, perfection, routines, focus.

Backups & sync

AES-256 zero-knowledge encryption (100k PBKDF2), Gzip compression, CSV export, and lossless Last-Write-Wins merge.

  • Encrypted: .enc.json with random passkeys.
  • Portable: CSV for Sheets / Excel / Python.
●  Private by design

Your data never leaves your pocket

No accounts, no cloud, no telemetry. Just a local Drift SQLite database you fully own.

Fully offline

Timer, streaks, reflections, badges — every feature works in airplane mode. Zero network calls.

Encrypted backups

AES-256-GCM with 100,000 PBKDF2 iterations. .json.gz archives shrink files ~90%.

Zero telemetry

No ads, no analytics SDKs, no tracking beacons. Export CSV or merge devices losslessly.
