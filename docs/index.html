App Showcase

Explore the clean Material 3 interface optimized for one-handed operation and clear visual hierarchy.

Daily Routine Tracker

The central dashboard gives you an immediate overview of daily progress with an interactive circular progress indicator, quick date stepper, category filter chips, and 48dp tappable check-in targets.

  • Completion Ring: Live daily percentage and cumulative XP earned.
  • Target Support: Checkbox, numeric delta steppers (+5, +10), and sub-day slots.
  • Quick Focus: One-tap launch directly into deep work timer sessions.

7-Day Adherence Matrix

View full weekly adherence across all active habits on an ISO Monday through Sunday grid. Quickly identify completion patterns, protected shield days, and scheduled days off.

  • 7-Day Adherence: Monday to Sunday consistency grid with clear visual status.
  • Shield Indicators: Clear visual cues for frozen grace days.
  • Quick Action: Long-press any date cell to apply or remove streak shields.

Circular Focus Timer

Distraction-free focus sessions integrated directly with habit goals. Features ambient noise playback, Do Not Disturb automation, and persistent notification service.

  • Background Service: Uninterrupted countdown when multitasking or screen is locked.
  • Ambient Sounds: Rain, White Noise, Cafe, and Forest sound generators.
  • Delta Adjustments: Add/subtract minutes (+5m, -5m) dynamically.

Comprehensive Habit Detail

Deep-dive into individual habit performance with interactive year/month calendar heatmaps, streak statistics, best streak records, and total completion volume.

  • Calendar Heatmap: GitHub-style density view of historical consistency.
  • Streak Engine: Day/week unit tracking, best streaks, and active streaks.
  • Shield Toggle: Direct manual freeze protection toggle per date.

Habit Shield Banking

A shield is a cork you earned. Bank them by staying consistent, then cork a sick or travel day so the shelf does not spill.

  • 14-Day Banking: Earn 1 shield for every 14 distinct days completed.
  • Automatic Rollover: Missed days freeze automatically if shields are available.
  • Audit History: Transparent event ledger of all earned and consumed shields.

Multi-Dimensional Analytics

Track long-term habit velocity through rolling adherence curves, completion rates, category breakdowns, and focus session volume.

  • Trend Curves: 7-day and 30-day rolling adherence trajectory.
  • Category Breakdown: Distribution of routines by life domain.
  • Total Volume: Total logged hours, check-ins, and active streak counts.

Mastery & Badges

XP, titles from Novice to Grandmaster, streak multipliers, and unlockable badges — ranks in keeping the shelf, not arcade noise.

  • Quadratic XP: Balanced leveling curve that values consistent discipline.
  • Streak Multiplier: Up to 2.0x XP multiplier for 30+ day streaks.
  • Achievement Showcase: Over 20 milestone badges rewarding dedication.
